# Privacy

retro-loader runs entirely on your own machine.

- It reads the conversation as it streams (your prompt, Claude's thinking and text, and tool names) only to draw the latest lines on the loading screen on your screen.
- It does not store, log or write any of that text, and does not send it anywhere.
- It makes no network requests and collects no analytics or telemetry.
- On Windows and Linux it passes one thing to a local audio player on the same machine: the tape sound it generates itself. No conversation text goes with it.

landrucode receives no data from the plugin.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/landrucode/retro-loader/issues or email dev@landrucode.ai.
